# ROMPE — design explainer

ROMPE is “Albion Online inside Ragnarok Online”: classic jobs, monsters and cards meet a player economy where choosing a place means choosing risk. The design argues that nostalgia alone is a narrow market, while hunting, crafting, trade and loss give players reasons to return. ORO adds these as layers over classic rules. This is an accepted design plan, not a claim that the whole sandbox is shipped.

- 1 · Choose your goal: Want any recipe. The Ledger shows how to reach it.
- 2 · Risk is a place: Know what drops before crossing a coloured border.
- 3 · Players create demand: Hunt, craft, trade, lose and replace. Selling is a valid non-death branch.
- 4 · Bring your people: Account tags, temporary parties and permanent guilds; event sides override during wars.
- 5 · Learn by doing: A fast planned start shows the future. Classic combat remains the foundation.
